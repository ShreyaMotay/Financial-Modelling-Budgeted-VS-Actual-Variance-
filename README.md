## 📘 Overview

This project simulates a retail business's financial performance by creating:
- A **12-month budget forecast**
- An **Actuals vs Budget variance analysis**
- A **dynamic Excel dashboard** with KPIs and trend charts

It is designed to replicate the core responsibilities of an FP&A Analyst.

---

## ⭐ Features

- Driver-based **Assumptions tab**
- Complete **Budget sheet** with revenue, COGS, profit calculations
- **Actuals & Variance** comparison using absolute and % variance formulas
- Professional **Excel dashboard** with:
  - Revenue trend (Budget vs Actual)
  - Net Profit trend
  - Revenue Variance %
  - Profit Variance (absolute + percentage)
  - YTD Budget vs Actual KPIs

---

## 🧮 Model Components

### **1. Assumptions (Drivers)**
Includes:
- Selling price
- Units sold
- Discount rate
- Returns rate
- COGS per unit
- Operating expenses
- Tax rate

These values drive the entire forecast.

---

### **2. Budget Sheet**
Calculates:
- Gross Revenue  
- Discounts  
- Net Revenue  
- Returns  
- Final Revenue  
- COGS  
- Gross Profit  
- Operating Profit  
- Net Profit  

Uses standard FP&A formulas throughout the 12-month period.

---

### **3. Actuals & Variance Analysis**
Tracks:
- Actual Revenue and Actual Net Profit
- Revenue Variance (Actual - Budget)
- Revenue Variance %
- Profit Variance
- Profit Variance %

The model automatically updates all variance calculations based on user input.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

Through analysis of actual vs budget performance:
- Revenue exceeded budget in 7 out of 12 months.
- Profit variance was positive in strong sales months.
- May–June showed performance dips due to lower revenue and fixed operating costs.
- Year-end revenue closed above budget expectations.
